# Drills for common weaknesses

## How to use these drills
Pick one or two drills that match your top fixes, not all of them. Do each in short sets (for example 3 to 5 sets of 10 to 20 repetitions or 30 to 45 seconds), with rest between sets so quality stays high. Film one set and compare it with the next session. The ShuttleSense rule each drill targets is named so you can check whether its pass rate improves.

## Shadow overheads for high contact
Targets `contact_above_head` (and helps `elbow_extension`). Without a shuttle, stand side-on at the back of the court, step behind an imaginary shuttle and swing so the racket meets it at full reach above and slightly in front of the racket shoulder. Then add a partner feeding high clears: the cue is "get behind it, hit it at the top". Count how many contacts feel high and in front.

## Throw and reach for a straight arm
Targets `elbow_extension`. Throw shuttles or a light ball overhead as far as you can from a side-on stance: a good throw needs the same elbow lead and full extension as a clear. Then hit fed clears focusing on reaching up with a nearly straight arm at contact. If the elbow stays bent, start the movement earlier so you are not late under the shuttle.

## Early drop contact
Targets `contact_above_shoulder`. A partner feeds medium-high lifts; you move back quickly and play drops while the shuttle is still above your head, aiming to land them in front of the short service line. Move your recovery start earlier each set; a late drop is taken low and floats.

## Soft drop feeding
Targets `short_follow_through`. From a fed lift, play a drop with a shortened swing that stops soon after contact, as if catching the shuttle on the strings. Place a target (a towel or cones) just over the net and score how many land on it. Alternate with a full clear swing to keep the disguise.

## Lunge ladder
Targets `lunge_knee_bend`. From base, lunge to each front corner onto the racket-side leg, heel first, front knee bent over the foot, chest up, then push back to base. Do sets of 10 per side. Add a partner feeding net shots once the lunge is stable. Stop if any joint hurts.

## Net reach drill
Targets `racket_arm_forward`. Racket up and in front, reach toward the net before your foot lands and meet the fed shuttle near the tape. A partner throws shuttles just over the net at varied heights; the cue is "racket first, then the foot". Score tight net replies.

## Lift under the shuttle
Targets `wrist_below_shoulder`. A partner feeds drops; lunge and get the racket under the shuttle, contact below shoulder height, and lift high to the back line. Mark the back 1 metre of the court and count how many lifts land there.

## Low serve practice
Targets `contact_below_waist` and serve consistency. Serve 20 low serves in a row aiming to skim the tape and land near the front service line. Keep the contact low and the swing short. Put a string or a second net tape a little above the net to show how high your serves travel.

## Serve stance routine
Targets `stable_feet`. Before every practice serve, set both feet, pause for a breath, then serve without moving either foot until the shuttle has been struck. Do 3 sets of 10 serves, ideally filmed so you can check that your feet stay still.

## Target zones for fewer errors
Targets `shots_out` (rallies ending with your shot out). Mark target zones about half a metre inside the side and back lines. Play clears, lifts and smashes to the zones from feeding or in a half-court game, counting shots that land in the zone versus out. Aim inside the lines when you are not under pressure.

## Split step timing
For footwork that is late to the shuttle. Shadow from base: hop and land with the feet slightly apart exactly when a partner calls "hit" or mimics a stroke, then move to the corner they point to. Progress to live rallies, focusing on landing as the opponent strikes.

## Six-corner shadow footwork
For general movement and recovery. From base, move to the six points (two front corners, two mid-court sides, two rear corners) in a called or random order, play a shadow stroke at each, and recover fully to base. Do 30 to 45 second sets with equal rest.

## Doubles rotation drill
For doubles pairs. One feeder at the net plays a lift or a short shot; the pair moves between front-and-back (attack) and side-by-side (defence) on each feed while calling "mine" or "yours". Start slow, then speed up.

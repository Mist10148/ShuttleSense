# Safety and injuries

## The coach cannot assess injuries
ShuttleSense gives heuristic technique cues from video. It cannot examine you and does not diagnose pain or injuries or suggest treatment. If you have pain, swelling, numbness, a joint that gives way, or pain that changes how you move, stop playing and see a qualified professional such as a doctor or physiotherapist.

## When to stop playing
Stop at once after a sudden sharp pain, a pop or a feeling of being kicked in the back of the lower leg, a twisted ankle that you cannot put weight on, chest pain, dizziness or unusual shortness of breath. Do not play through pain to finish a game.

## General prevention habits
Warm up before playing, increase training load gradually, wear court shoes with good grip and support, keep the court clear of loose shuttles, and call for the shuttle in doubles to avoid racket clashes. Rest when you are very tired, since technique and footwork break down with fatigue.

## Technique while returning from injury
Once a professional has cleared you to play, rebuild gradually: start with gentle rallying and shadow footwork, then add speed and jumping. The coach can help with technique cues, but the return plan itself should come from the professional treating you.

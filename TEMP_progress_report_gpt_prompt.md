## Project context

- ShuttleSense is an in-development badminton video-analysis system. Its Flutter client supports workflows such as connection, uploading, processing, viewing results, court calibration, live sessions, and coaching; a Python/FastAPI backend coordinates computer vision, analysis, and video output.
- The documented processing architecture includes TrackNetV3 for shuttle tracking, YOLOX for player detection, RTMPose for pose estimation, court homography, hit detection, stroke classification, analysis, and annotated video rendering. The shared clip record feeds downstream analysis and coaching. The precise models actually enabled depend on the configuration and environment.
- The court minimap can display a player-position coverage heatmap. Its reliability depends on accurate player positions and court mapping; do not confuse it with TrackNet's model-output heatmaps.
- Coaching includes rule-based feedback, session-level improvement notes and summaries, and a coach-chat workflow based on a text digest of the session record when the backend is configured. Avoid claiming that the chat itself trains a model or that it provides professional medical advice.
